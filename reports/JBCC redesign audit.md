# Make JBCC’s public record actionable

The redesign now presents JBCC as an industry council with a documented mandate, useful records, member bodies, seminars, and a clear Secretariat route, replacing generic contractor-template messaging with source-based content. Its hero offers a visitor-selected, keyboard-operable route panel and uses a brief motion-safe fade instead of an auto-rotating carousel. The media and document transfer is substantial but incomplete: the anonymous media API reported 359 attachments and exposed 276 records; 275 files downloaded, while one returned 404, and only seven of nine public document entries produced PDFs. The captured source set is preserved in the project, with four selected images and seven PDFs used by the site. This is a dated, curated snapshot rather than a live synchronization with WordPress, and some source claims and reuse rights still need JBCC confirmation before launch.

## Preserve the council’s evidence and exclude polluted content

The public site’s strongest material is specific and useful: it says JBCC was founded in 1980 by AAK and KABCEC, describes representation across owners and developers, consultants, and contractors, and lists objectives around industry discussion, shared contract procedures, price-fluctuation information, and dispute resolution. The redesign carries that institutional material into the homepage, About page, and “What we do” page. ([About JBCC](https://jbcc.or.ke/about-us/); [Services and objectives](https://jbcc.or.ke/services/); [local home page](../src/routes/index.tsx); [local data](../src/lib/jbcc-data.ts))

The source sitemap is not a safe content feed by itself. At the 8 October 2026 capture, it listed 50 pages, 466 posts, and nine document packages; many post titles were unrelated betting and casino SEO spam, while other routes were theme demos, stale pages, or unrelated commerce entries. The live homepage also used generic contractor copy. Those records should remain excluded from JBCC editorial content unless the Secretariat verifies them. A future content-sync process should allow-list approved institutional routes and document packages, preserve source dates and links, and flag unexpected changes for review instead of importing the entire WordPress API. ([WordPress sitemap index](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-sitemap.xml); [post sitemap](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-sitemap-posts-post-1.xml); [live homepage](https://jbcc.or.ke/))

The redesigned routes capture the source’s stated objectives, constituent bodies, contact details, and seminar subjects, but current operational claims need confirmation. The live contact page lists Suite 605, Blue Violets Plaza, Kamburu Drive off Ngong Road, Nairobi, `mail@jbcc.or.ke`, and two phone numbers; the seminar page names the Principal Building Agreement, Nominated/Selected Subcontract Agreement, Minor Works Agreement, and contract administration methods, but no upcoming dates were found. The redesign appropriately directs visitors to the Secretariat for dates and registration. Its governance page omits the previous named roster because the local list conflicts with later public appointment information and no complete, dated current roster was found. Confirm contact channels, partner names, and board roles with JBCC before publication. ([Contact page](https://jbcc.or.ke/contact-us/); [Seminars page](https://jbcc.or.ke/seminars/); [AAK AGM Report 2025](https://aak.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/AAK-AGM-Report-2025_compressed.pdf); [local About page](../src/routes/about.tsx))

## Treat the archive as a best-effort snapshot

The archive contains 275 successfully retrieved media files and the seven valid PDFs returned from the nine public WP Download Manager entries. Media ID 9655 (`Full-video_2.jpeg`) returned 404. The Building Code 2006 package said no file was attached, and the September 2022 price-index package said its file was not found; both HTML responses are preserved as package-page evidence rather than mislabeled as documents. The public directory contains four selected source visuals—three photos and the JBCC logo—and seven PDFs. The complete retrieved set, manifest, failed requests, and scope notes are in [`site-source-archive/`](../site-source-archive/README.md) and [`manifest.json`](../site-source-archive/manifest.json); only the selected subset is served from [`public/media/`](../public/media/) and [`public/documents/`](../public/documents/).

The gap is material: the API reported 359 media records but anonymous pagination exposed only 276, leaving 83 reported records unexplained. The archive therefore cannot be described as a full copy of the source site’s media or files. It also contains old theme and demo assets; every item needs a relevance, provenance, and rights check before reuse. The seven locally listed documents are historical material. The newest valid price index captured is June 2022; although a September 2022 catalogue entry exists, its file was unavailable. The package transfer verified downloadable PDF responses and stored names and sizes, but did not independently review every PDF’s contents. The redesign labels the index status and dates older FAQ statements about quarterly indices, issue frequency, and Green Book prices as historical rather than current. ([media API count](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/media?per_page=1); anonymous capture pages [1](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/media?per_page=100&page=1), [2](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/media?per_page=100&page=2), [3](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/media?per_page=100&page=3), and [4](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/media?per_page=100&page=4); [document sitemap](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-sitemap-posts-wpdmpro-1.xml); [FAQ](https://jbcc.or.ke/frequently-asked-questions/); [local resource page](../src/routes/resources.tsx))

For a reliable public archive, add a named JBCC content owner, an approval and rights field for each asset, and a review date for each document entry. Each listing should show title, issuing body, edition/date, file type, source URL, and whether JBCC confirms it as current or historical. Reconcile the source catalogue against the local manifest on an agreed schedule, log missing or changed files, and ask JBCC before removing or replacing records. Do not publish historical prices or a publication cadence as current without confirmation.

## Make the hero guide visitors into real work

The implemented hero has a clear, factual headline, local construction imagery, architectural linework, and three user-selected destinations for documents, seminars, and the council. Selection is made with native buttons and `aria-pressed`; the selected panel has a short motion-safe fade and no automatic rotation. The headline remains visible at first paint. This is a more meaningful invitation to explore than a generic slogan, counters, a stock-photo carousel, or abstract animated decoration. ([home page implementation](../src/routes/index.tsx); [GOV.UK image guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/images/); [NN/G homepage guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/113-design-guidelines-homepage/))

Keep the next iteration anchored in actual JBCC records. Let each route selector reveal a dated image or document detail, one factual sentence, and a direct link to the corresponding page. Expand the journey with descriptive section links—history, objectives, constituent bodies, resources, seminars, and contact—while keeping normal scrolling in the visitor’s control. Use a real project or meeting photograph only with an identified date, place, creator, and reuse permission; otherwise, prefer a locally authored line drawing or document detail. A useful archive pattern is the Canadian Centre for Architecture’s contextual cataloguing across drawings, photographs, artefacts, audiovisual and digital records; the Aga Khan Award’s project finder also demonstrates practical filters by place, year, and building type. Adapt these discovery patterns to JBCC documents and their real metadata instead of copying another institution’s visual identity. ([CCA collection](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/about-collection); [CCA archives](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/archives/); [AKAA Project Finder](https://the.akdn/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture-project-finder); [RIBA Awards](https://www.riba.org/explore/awards/))

GSAP is not currently installed. If the team adds it, use `@gsap/react`’s scoped `useGSAP()` for one short hero entrance: reveal the image, draw a few SVG strokes, and settle the supporting label and destination panel. Keep the main heading static and readable immediately. Use `gsap.matchMedia()` for viewport and `prefers-reduced-motion` branches, and let the hook revert animation work on route teardown; any event-triggered GSAP animation should use its context-safe pattern. Preserve the project’s shared editorial text, image, and line wrappers rather than adding a second animation controller to those same elements. ([GSAP React guide](https://gsap.com/resources/React/); [GSAP matchMedia](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia/); [project motion principles](../docs/design-principles.md))

React Bits is better treated as a source of one restrained visual idea than as a default effect layer. A faint Topography or Grid Scan motif could sit behind a real image or authored drawing, but inspect the chosen implementation for rendering cost, keyboard behavior, reduced-motion handling, and content visibility before adopting it. Avoid continuous full-screen Canvas/WebGL, cursor trails, glitch text, parallax, and overlapping animation ownership. The Staggered Text component is documented on a Pro site, so confirm its availability and terms separately; for components copied from the main repository, retain required license notices and verify the current license before shipping. Any automatically moving effect that runs for more than five seconds alongside other content needs a pause, stop, or hide mechanism under WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2; also respect reduced-motion preferences and make interaction work with keyboard and touch. ([React Bits component index](https://reactbits.dev/get-started/index); [React Bits license](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/LICENSE.md); [W3C Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide); [W3C Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions); [MDN reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/%40media/prefers-reduced-motion))

## Close the launch gaps with owners and checks

The immediate decisions are about source accuracy and operating ownership, not adding more effects. The redesign’s selected imagery comes from the source media archive, but the source does not identify all pictured people, dates, photographers, or usage rights. The archive should remain outside the served bundle except for explicitly selected, reviewed assets. A short research round with architects, contractors, consultants, students, and public-sector users should validate whether “find a document,” “check an index,” “understand JBCC,” or “contact the Secretariat” deserves the most prominent entry point; this is an untested hierarchy, not a measured engagement result. GOV.UK recommends starting with observed user needs, while NN/G’s information-scent guidance supports descriptive labels that let people predict where a link leads. ([GOV.UK user-needs guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs); [NN/G information scent](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/information-scent/))

| Priority | Finding | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | The local copy is static and curated, not a live feed; the source catalogue contains spam/demo records. | Have JBCC approve an allow-list of institutional routes and define who reviews updates. |
| Launch | The media count is incomplete, one exposed file failed, and two catalogue entries had no usable PDF. | Keep the capture labelled best-effort; ask JBCC for the missing media and document files. |
| Launch | Rights and image provenance are not established for every captured visual. | Confirm permission, creator, date, place, and names before publication; replace unverified photos with authored diagrams if needed. |
| Launch | Current board, contact details, member-body naming, seminar dates, prices, and document currency need confirmation. | Request a dated Secretariat-approved content sheet; keep unsupported board names and stale prices off the site. |
| Before release | The required Playwright end-to-end suite could not be run because Bun and project dependencies are absent in this workspace. | Install the declared dependencies in the approved environment and run `bun run test:e2e`, especially across phone/tablet navigation and metadata behavior. |
| After release | No visitor interviews, search analytics, performance profile, or accessibility review informed this pass. | Test common tasks, keyboard operation, reduced-motion changes, screen-reader announcements, and a modest-phone performance profile. |

The source capture and implementation should be treated as a strong editorial baseline, not a complete source migration. The live site’s sitemap and public APIs can support a reviewed import, but they do not establish freshness, rights, or completeness. ([GOV.UK user-needs guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs); [public sitemap](https://jbcc.or.ke/wp-sitemap.xml))

## Conclusion

The redesign is more specific to JBCC because its content now leads with documented institutional work and practical routes rather than generic construction marketing. Its credibility depends on preserving the date and source context of every record, keeping the incomplete archive honestly labelled, and assigning a JBCC owner to verify current content and permissions. Add motion only where it clarifies a real record or next step; the present selectable hero already provides a useful interaction without auto-play.
## Expanded design inspiration research

A broader review of Kenyan professional bodies, engineering and arbitration organizations, architecture collections, and African cultural archives reinforces the redesign’s task-first direction. AAK uses direct member/event/certificate paths and categorized resources; Ciarb and ICE organize guidance and learning by audience, topic, and format; the Aga Khan Award, CCA, Accra Architecture Archive, and Digital Benin connect records to places, provenance, and context. Adapt those information patterns to JBCC’s records and Kenyan construction context without copying another institution’s identity. The exact `superhero.io` website could not be verified, so no specific visual or interaction behavior is attributed to it. ([AAK homepage](https://aak.or.ke/); [Ciarb guidance](https://www.ciarb.org/resources/professional-practice-guidelines/international-arbitration/); [ICE Knowledge Hub](https://knowledgehub.ice.org.uk/); [AKAA Project Finder](https://the.akdn/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture-project-finder); [CCA Collection](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/about-collection); [Accra Architecture Archive](https://www.accraarchive.com/); [Digital Benin](https://digitalbenin.org/); [Superhero.io](https://superhero.io/))

For JBCC, prioritize four concrete homepage tasks, a searchable resource ledger with verified edition/status metadata, separate upcoming and past seminar pathways, and constituent-body descriptions tied to confirmed expertise or work. Consider a map, timeline, or archive story only when JBCC has rights-cleared records and reliable place/date metadata. GSAP or React Bits can add a single short line/image reveal or quiet drafting texture, while the shared reveal wrappers, reduced-motion behavior, keyboard/touch controls, and responsive asset loading remain central.
