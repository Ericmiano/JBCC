# Turn JBCC’s Real Records Into Exploration

A broader look at Kenyan professional bodies, engineering and arbitration organizations, architecture collections, and cultural archives points to a useful direction for JBCC: make genuine records and clear next steps the source of engagement. The exact `superhero.io` website could not be verified during the research session, and the closest primary-source match was a mobile game listing rather than a web-design case study. So this report does not attribute a particular visual style or web interaction to that domain. Instead, it translates transferable ideas—choice, progress, discovery, and context—into practical routes through JBCC’s documents, seminars, mandate, and constituent bodies. These examples show what institutions publish and how they structure content; they do not prove that a particular interaction raises engagement or return visits. Adapt their information patterns to JBCC’s Kenyan construction-contract context and visual identity, without copying another institution’s branding.

## The exact Superhero.io reference remains unverified

The research tools could not retrieve usable page content from `https://superhero.io/` or its `www` variant. The strongest primary-source name match was a Google Play listing for a LoraStudio mobile game; its support section points to a different developer website, and the listing describes game features such as character progression, tasks, rewards, and power-ups. That listing is evidence about the game product, not the requested website’s design, navigation, animation, responsiveness, or accessibility. No official design case study or implementation explanation for the exact domain was found. ([Superhero.io](https://superhero.io/); [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lorastudio.superhero2); [Tokgame site linked by the listing](https://tokgame.cn/))

The only cautious translation is the general idea of visible, user-directed progress: a visitor chooses a need and gets a meaningful next step. For JBCC, that could mean seeing whether a contract or index is available, what seminar information exists, or how to contact the Secretariat. This is a design inference, not a feature verified on Superhero.io. Game-like ranks, streaks, random rewards, combat imagery, or unlock mechanics would obscure the trust and predictability people need when looking for contract documents and professional guidance. Motion should clarify the route, not stand in for useful content. ([W3C: Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/animation-from-interactions); [W3C: Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide))

## Comparable institutions make real tasks easy to enter

AAK offers the closest Kenyan professional-body reference. Its homepage places tasks such as joining, finding events, and validating a certificate beside a “Find your fit” path that asks about discipline and career stage. Its resource centre groups material by recognizable type and purpose, while its events and accessibility pages expose separate routes for activities and accessibility information. These are useful structural examples: start with what a visitor wants to do, then show the institutional structure and archive. For JBCC, a direct “What do you need?” set of links can lead to a contract, price index, seminar, or Secretariat contact without asking first whether someone understands council committees. ([AAK homepage](https://aak.or.ke/); [AAK Resource Centre](https://aak.or.ke/resources); [AAK Events](https://aak.or.ke/events); [AAK Accessibility](https://aak.or.ke/accessibility))

Other professional organizations show how to explain specialized material before asking people to download it. Ciarb organizes professional-practice guidance by practice area, explains intended audiences, and provides direct download actions; its event listings expose event types, places, and dates. ICE’s Knowledge Hub labels content by subject, format, and estimated time, and its events area gives recorded lectures their own discovery route. RIBA exposes event categories and locations. Together these examples suggest that a resource title, its audience, its format, its date, and a plain-language explanation can make a catalogue more usable than a row of promotional tiles. JBCC should only expose filters for information it can keep current. ([Ciarb International Arbitration Guidelines](https://www.ciarb.org/resources/professional-practice-guidelines/international-arbitration/); [Ciarb Events](https://www.ciarb.org/events/); [ICE Knowledge Hub](https://knowledgehub.ice.org.uk/); [ICE Events](https://ice.org.uk/events); [RIBA What’s On](https://www.riba.org/whats-on/))

Architecture and cultural archives show a second useful pattern: connect a record to the people, place, or history that makes it understandable. The Aga Khan Award for Architecture’s Project Finder exposes search dimensions such as area, year, and building type. The Canadian Centre for Architecture describes collections through distinct archival types and contextual finding aids. Accra Architecture Archive links digitized records to city and people stories, while Digital Benin connects catalogue records with institutions, provenance, oral histories, maps, narratives, and archival sources. These are larger collections than JBCC’s present public file set, so their systems should not be copied wholesale. The transferable idea is smaller: pair each JBCC file with a source, edition/date, status, short purpose, and related document or seminar where that relationship is verified. ([Aga Khan Award Project Finder](https://the.akdn/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture-project-finder); [CCA Collection](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/about-collection); [Accra Architecture Archive](https://www.accraarchive.com/); [Digital Benin](https://digitalbenin.org/); [Digital Benin visual research tools](https://digitalbenin.org/archive/visual-research-tools))

## Let the homepage lead into useful journeys

The current JBCC hero already has a factual headline and visitor-selected destinations for documents, seminars, and the Council. The next design pass can make that selector more concrete: use buttons such as “Find a contract,” “Check an index,” “Explore a seminar,” and “Ask the Secretariat,” then show one verified record or small visual detail, a concise explanation, and a direct link. A still image or authored drawing can change with the selected route; keep the headline visible at first paint, do not rotate choices automatically, and keep the selection controls available as ordinary keyboard- and touch-operable buttons. This builds on AAK’s need-based pathway and the existing JBCC interaction rather than importing a game interface. ([AAK homepage](https://aak.or.ke/); [current JBCC homepage implementation](../src/routes/index.tsx))

After the hero, guide visitors through a short editorial sequence: what JBCC does, which bodies it convenes, which resources are available, what seminar subjects it covers, and how to contact the Secretariat. Use section links and asymmetric editorial layouts so the page has a clear rhythm without repeating identical cards. A small index or progress cue may help people see what follows, but normal scrolling should remain in their control. RIBA’s curated architecture stories and the Library of Congress’s multimedia Story Maps show that a collection can support a narrative route through real records; a JBCC version could explain how a particular agreement, advisory note, or seminar relates to a practical question, provided the Secretariat confirms the relationship and rights. ([RIBA and Google Arts & Culture](https://www.architecture.com/explore-architecture/google-arts-and-culture); [Library of Congress Story Maps](https://www.loc.gov/research-centers/african-and-middle-eastern/researcher-resources/story-maps/); [GOV.UK user-needs guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs))

The resource centre should become the site’s strongest functional interaction. Begin with a compact, searchable list grouped by contract forms, price indices, guidance, and historical material. Show the edition or date, source, format, availability, and a clear status such as “historical,” “file unavailable,” or “current—confirmed [date]”; do not call a file current unless JBCC verifies it. Link related materials only where the relationship is known. The current capture has seven valid PDFs and two unavailable package entries, and its newest valid price index is from June 2022, so a “latest” section must not imply present currency until the Secretariat supplies an approved catalogue. The National Construction Authority’s building-code page also demonstrates the value of presenting a primary code alongside subject-specific sections, while AAK groups materials into practical categories. ([AAK Resource Centre](https://aak.or.ke/resources); [NCA National Building Code](https://www.nca.go.ke/building-code); [JBCC local resource page](../src/routes/resources.tsx))

For seminars, distinguish confirmed upcoming dates from a list of topics or past events. If JBCC can maintain the data, show topic, date and time, venue or online format, audience, registration deadline, and a direct registration/contact route. Add CPD status, price, speakers, recordings, or presentation files only when confirmed. A past seminar can remain useful through a short note or downloadable presentation, but only when JBCC has permission to publish it. In the member section, show constituent bodies and the expertise they bring; connect them to verified work or seminar contributions. Keep named representatives off the page until JBCC provides a dated, approved roster. A map, timeline, or project explorer is a later option only if the Council has reliable records with actual locations or dates; UNESCO’s Urban Heritage Atlas and RIBA’s city model demonstrate richer place-based storytelling, but JBCC should not invent a spatial dataset to imitate them. ([Ciarb Events](https://www.ciarb.org/events/); [AAK Events](https://aak.or.ke/events); [UNESCO Urban Heritage Atlas](https://whc.unesco.org/en/urban-heritage-atlas/); [RIBA Exhibitions](https://www.old.architecture.com/explore-architecture/exhibitions))

## Use motion as a restrained editorial cue

GSAP can choreograph one brief entrance using a real JBCC photograph or authored SVG: draw a few structural lines, reveal a caption, then settle. For the hero destination buttons, use a small opacity or image transition after a visitor’s choice. Keep the headline and controls readable without waiting for animation, and do not pin the whole hero, force scrolling, auto-advance the selector, or attach multiple animation systems to the same elements. GSAP’s React guide documents scoped setup and cleanup through `useGSAP()`, and `gsap.matchMedia()` can create and revert variants for viewport and reduced-motion conditions. In this repository, use the shared text, line, and image reveal wrappers required by the project instructions; do not add a competing motion owner to their elements. ([GSAP React guide](https://gsap.com/resources/React/); [GSAP matchMedia](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia/); [project motion principles](../docs/design-principles.md))

React Bits is best used as an idea source for a subdued architectural surface, not a collection of effects to layer over every section. Its component index lists Topography and Grid Scan backgrounds, which could inspire a static, low-contrast plan-grid texture behind a genuine image or locally authored drawing. The retrieved catalogue does not establish the implementation cost, keyboard behavior, or reduced-motion behavior of those specific backgrounds, so inspect the exact component before adopting it. The Staggered Text documentation exposes a reduced-motion option, but it is hosted in the Pro documentation; check availability and terms for the chosen installation path. Prefer one effect at most in a view, and avoid cursor trails, glitch/scramble text, generic particles, and continuous WebGL when they do not reveal JBCC information. ([React Bits component index](https://reactbits.dev/get-started/index); [React Bits repository](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits); [Staggered Text documentation](https://pro.reactbits.dev/docs/components/staggered-text); [React Bits license](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/LICENSE.md))

All essential text and destinations should remain available if motion is disabled or a script fails. W3C says WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2 requires a pause, stop, or hide mechanism for qualifying automatically moving content that runs for more than five seconds alongside other content; SC 2.3.3 describes disabling non-essential interaction-triggered animation at Level AAA. These are different criteria, so a short entrance does not remove the value of honoring reduced motion. Use the site’s centralized reduced-motion CSS and shared wrappers, make controls work by keyboard and touch, provide visible focus, and keep any map’s information available in a plain text/list form. Serve responsive image sizes, lazy-load off-screen media, and only load a video after a visitor asks to play it; web.dev notes that `preload="auto"` downloads a full video even when the visitor never presses play. ([W3C: Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide); [W3C: Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html); [MDN: Reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/%40media/prefers-reduced-motion); [web.dev: Responsive images](https://web.dev/articles/serve-responsive-images); [web.dev: Video basics](https://web.dev/articles/video-basics))

## Prioritize useful discovery before richer effects

The strongest first step is content ownership: have the Secretariat confirm which documents are current, which prices and seminar dates may be published, how the constituent bodies should be named, and which photographs JBCC has the right to reuse. Then make the visitor paths and resource catalogue reflect that verified data. The examples below describe a practical sequence for this project; effort and risk are estimates, not measured vendor estimates.

| Priority | JBCC opportunity | Fit and effort | Main dependency or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Refine the hero choices into four direct tasks with a factual preview and link. | High fit; low effort. It extends the current selector and AAK’s task-first entry pattern ([AAK homepage](https://aak.or.ke/)). | Keep the choice set tied to real pages and data; no auto-rotation. |
| 2 | Build a searchable resource ledger with date, edition, source, format, status, and related links. | High fit; medium effort. AAK and Ciarb show useful resource organization ([AAK Resource Centre](https://aak.or.ke/resources); [Ciarb guidance](https://www.ciarb.org/resources/professional-practice-guidelines/international-arbitration/)). | Requires Secretariat verification; the current captured PDFs are historic/incomplete. |
| 3 | Separate upcoming seminars from past learning resources; add filters only for fields JBCC maintains. | High fit; low-to-medium effort. ICE and professional-body event examples show date/topic/type routes ([ICE Events](https://ice.org.uk/events); [Ciarb Events](https://www.ciarb.org/events/)). | Do not imply dates, CPD credit, fees, or registration availability without confirmation. |
| 4 | Connect constituent-body representation to verified expertise and activities. | Medium-high fit; medium effort. AAK’s chapter structure makes professional representation legible ([AAK About Us](https://aak.or.ke/about)). | Needs approved names, descriptions, and dated governance information; avoid unverified portraits. |
| 5 | Add contextual stories, maps, or timelines for real places/documents. | Conditional fit; high effort. AKAA, CCA, Accra Archive, Digital Benin, UNESCO, and RIBA show archive-led exploration ([AKAA Project Finder](https://the.akdn/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture/aga-khan-award-for-architecture-project-finder); [CCA Collection](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/about-collection); [Accra Archive](https://www.accraarchive.com/); [Digital Benin](https://digitalbenin.org/); [UNESCO Atlas](https://whc.unesco.org/en/urban-heritage-atlas/); [RIBA Exhibitions](https://www.old.architecture.com/explore-architecture/exhibitions)). | Needs a curated, rights-cleared corpus and verified geographic or historical metadata; not a launch dependency. |
| 6 | Add one short GSAP or React Bits flourish. | Medium fit; low-to-medium effort, with accessibility/performance review ([GSAP React guide](https://gsap.com/resources/React/); [React Bits index](https://reactbits.dev/get-started/index)). | Inspect exact code, license, reduced-motion behavior, and device cost; shared wrappers remain authoritative. |

These patterns create engagement through evidence and useful choices: a visitor arrives with a need, recognizes the relevant resource, understands its status, and sees what to do next. That model fits JBCC’s current information better than a large 3D landing scene or a museum-scale map. The research did not measure user behavior on the reference sites, so validate the task order with a small set of architects, contractors, consultants, students, and public-sector users before treating it as settled. ([GOV.UK user-needs guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs); [NN/G: Information Scent](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/information-scent/))

## Conclusion

The useful lesson from these institutions is that exploration grows from strong content connections: an audience can enter by need, documents carry enough metadata to be trusted, events link to their learning materials, and member bodies are tied to real work. JBCC can make its existing hero feel more dynamic through user choice and a short image or line transition, while keeping the central experience quick, legible, and grounded in verified records. The Superhero.io reference remains unresolved; no site-specific look or behavior should be attributed to it.
