# JBCC website rebuild

## Outcome
Build a complete multi-page JBCC website that preserves the institution’s navy, white, red, and Poppins identity while presenting it as a technical publication and professional resource centre.

## Pages and shared experience
- Create a shared architectural header, navigation, universal Cmd/Ctrl+K search overlay, and institutional footer.
- Build distinct routes for Home, About, What We Do, Resources, Events, and Contact.
- Give every page unique search and social metadata.
- Make layouts work across desktop and mobile with visible focus states and reduced-motion support.

## Home page
- Full-viewport technical hero with JBCC identity, measured grid lines, restrained red details, and source photography.
- Quick-access search and links to contracts, price indices, documents, publications, events, and contact.
- Editorial “Who we are” story using verified history and the supplied factual figures.
- Interactive five-item “What we do” list with changing contextual imagery.
- Filterable resource-centre preview with document viewing and downloads.
- News and events, Price Adjustment Formula section, eight constituent bodies, and full Secretariat contact area.

## Dedicated pages
- **About:** history since 1980, governance, constituent bodies, and memorandum objectives.
- **What We Do:** detailed institutional objectives and activities.
- **Resources:** faceted document filtering by category and year, previews, direct source downloads, and future-ready quarterly organization.
- **Events:** dated event and seminar listings, venue/schedule details where source-verified, CPD information, and registration enquiry links.
- **Contact:** Secretariat details, map/location link, and a validated enquiry form that opens the visitor’s email client without requiring data storage.

## Content and media
- Research jbcc.or.ke and use only source-verified organizational information, news, event details, addresses, and downloadable files.
- Download relevant source media into the project rather than hotlinking it.
- Preserve source URLs for documents so view/download actions remain functional.
- Avoid fabricated statistics, testimonials, events, or imagery.

## Visual system
- Use JBCC Navy `#030F27`, JBCC Red `#EA1C25`, off-white and white surfaces, charcoal accents, and Poppins.
- Favor square/subtle edges, rules, numbering, metadata, asymmetry, photography, and ledger-like document rows.
- Avoid gradients, glass effects, oversized rounded cards, generic corporate compositions, icon clutter, and excessive red CTAs.
- Add restrained transitions, sticky-header behavior, image movement, and accessible reveal effects without scroll-jacking.

## Verification
- Check source links and document actions.
- Verify search, filters, navigation, form behavior, and interactive “What we do” states.
- Inspect desktop and mobile rendering for overlap, readability, accessibility, and loading errors.
